FAQ

Frequently Asked Tax Questions

What documents are required for income tax return filing?

Required documents depend on your income sources and circumstances. Common records can include PAN, Aadhaar where applicable, Form 16 or income records, bank details, investment proofs, tax payment information and supporting records for deductions or other income.

Do you provide ITR filing services across India?

Yes. MG Tax Consultant supports income tax return preparation and filing for eligible individuals, professionals and businesses across India, based on the facts, records and filing requirements applicable to each case.

Can you help with GST registration and return filing?

Yes. Assistance can cover GST registration documentation, process support, record review and return filing support. Whether registration is required depends on the applicable facts and law.

Can you help with income tax or GST notices?

Yes. Official communications should be reviewed carefully and within the relevant time limits. Support can include understanding the communication, organising documents and preparing the response process without guaranteeing an outcome.

Do you provide accounting and bookkeeping services?

Yes. Services can include bookkeeping, ledger organisation, transaction records and compliance-ready financial record support for businesses and professionals.
