Common GST Filing Mistakes Businesses Should Avoid

A simple prevention-focused checklist for recurring GST compliance.

Published 13 September 2026 • General information only
Important: Tax and compliance rules depend on current law and individual facts. This guide is informational and should not be treated as personalised advice.

Filing from incomplete books

Submitting returns before sales and purchase records are complete can make later reconciliation more difficult.

Ignoring document mismatches

Differences in invoices, tax values or party details should be reviewed instead of carried forward without explanation.

Waiting until the last moment

Late record collection increases the risk of missing documents and leaves less time to resolve questions.

Treating every period as isolated

Opening issues from prior periods can affect current compliance. Keep a list of unresolved items and review them systematically.

Not maintaining evidence

Retain invoices, payment records and working papers needed to support the figures reported, subject to applicable retention rules.

When professional review can help

If the facts are unclear, records are incomplete, a deadline is approaching or the issue could materially affect tax or compliance, obtain professional advice based on the actual documents and current requirements.
