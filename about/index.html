Who we are

About MG Tax Consultant

MG Tax Consultant is an Amritsar-based tax and compliance consultancy focused on helping clients understand and manage their tax, GST, TDS, accounting and business compliance responsibilities. The practice serves local clients in Amritsar and supports individuals, professionals and businesses across India where consultation and document coordination can be handled remotely.

Our objective is to make tax and compliance work more organised, understandable and manageable. Rather than relying on one-size-fits-all answers, each matter should be reviewed against the client’s facts, records, deadlines and the law applicable at that time.

FAQ

About MG Tax Consultant — Frequently Asked Questions

Where is MG Tax Consultant based?

MG Tax Consultant is based in Amritsar, Punjab, India. Local consultation and Local SEO are centred on Amritsar.

Do you support clients outside Amritsar?

Yes. Where the service can be handled appropriately through remote consultation and document coordination, support may be provided to clients across India.

What services do you provide?

Core service areas include income tax and ITR filing, GST, TDS compliance, accounting and bookkeeping, tax notice assistance, tax planning and business-registration support.

Can all tax matters be handled remotely?

Not necessarily. Suitability depends on the matter, documents, applicable process and whether any physical verification, appearance or specialist involvement is required.

No. Tax positions and outcomes depend on the facts, documentation, applicable law and decisions or processing by the relevant authorities.
